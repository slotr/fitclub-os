Frequently asked

Ten things buyers always ask. Direct answers.

Does this work for my gym in Türkiye?
Yes. Native ₺ pricing on every plan and invoice. Iyzico and PayTR integrations on the Q2 2026 roadmap (today we charge via Stripe; ₺ payouts work). KVKK compliance is built in, not bolted on — we do not capture biometric data and we host EU-region by default. Turkish UI (member app + admin) is on the roadmap; Turkish member-facing WhatsApp templates already ship.
What if I want to leave?
The 14-day pilot is no-questions walk-away — if you're not happier on day 15, we hand back your data and waive the bill. After the pilot there is no annual contract. Cancel any time with 7 days' notice. We export every byte of your data (members, payments, check-ins, conversations) within 24 hours of cancellation, in CSV + JSON.
How long is onboarding?
14 days from signed pilot to live members. Day 1–3 we migrate your member list, plan catalogue and active subscriptions. Day 4–7 we wire WhatsApp Business, Stripe and your custom branding. Day 8–14 we shadow you running classes and check-ins, fix whatever surfaces, and hand off the keys.
Can I keep using Glofox / Mindbody during the pilot?
Yes. We support parallel-run mode — your members keep using their existing tool while we shadow it on our side. On cut-over day you flip a single setting and we send the announcement WhatsApp blast. Your team isn't asked to stop the existing system before ours is proven.
What about my member data privacy?
KVKK and GDPR compliant. Data is processed in EU regions (Frankfurt + Istanbul edge). Sensitive columns are encrypted at rest with pgcrypto. Members get a working right-to-access export and right-to-erasure (30-day grace, then hard delete) inside the app — no support ticket needed.
Do you do hardware (turnstile, fingerprint, face)?
On purpose, no. Rotating QR replaces every job a turnstile does without the capex, installation, downtime and biometric-data liability. We refuse to build fingerprint or face check-in even if asked — it's a regulatory cliff and a member trust killer in our markets.
How does WhatsApp pricing work?
Pre-approved utility templates (booking confirmation, payment receipt, class reminder, check-in confirmation) are free. Conversations initiated by your members are free for the first 24h. Outside that, marketing messages cost ~$0.005–0.05 per message depending on country. We pass Meta's wholesale rate through at cost — no per-message markup.
Is there a setup fee?
No. Onboarding, migration and WhatsApp Business setup are included in the monthly price. No hidden activation, no per-member charge, no per-template fee.
Can I get a custom plan?
For 5+ locations, yes — multi-location operators get a custom contract with volume pricing, dedicated CSM, and an SLA. Reach out from the contact link below and we'll send a same-day quote.
